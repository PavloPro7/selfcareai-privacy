Selfcare Scan support

How can we help?

Find quick answers below or contact us directly. Include your app version and device model when reporting a technical problem.

Contact support

We’ll use your email only to respond to your request.

Email 03ppcv@gmail.com

Restore a purchase

  1. Open the Pro upgrade screen in Selfcare Scan.
  2. Select Restore Purchases.
  3. Use the same App Store or Google Play account used for the purchase.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by the store that processed the purchase. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel billing.

  • Manage an Apple subscription
  • Manage a Google Play subscription

Reset skin data

Open Account, select Reset skin data, and confirm. Your sign-in account stays active. See the full deletion details.

Delete your account

Open Account, select Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm. If you cannot access the app, email us from your account address. Never send your password.

Improve a photo check-in

  • Use even, natural light and avoid strong shadows.
  • Keep the camera steady and your face clearly visible.
  • For easier comparison, use similar lighting and distance each time.
  • If the camera is unavailable, choose a suitable photo from your library.

Analysis did not complete

Check your connection and try again. If it keeps happening, email your device model, operating-system version, app version, and the approximate time of the error. Do not send your password or a face photo unless you choose to include it.

Important

Cosmetic guidance, not medical care.

Selfcare Scan does not diagnose or treat health conditions. Seek qualified medical care for pain, spreading redness, infection, rapid changes, allergic reactions, suspicious lesions, or another health concern.
