Privacy Policy

Selfcare Scan does not sell personal data or run advertising. Full-size scan photos are used for analysis but are not kept in scan history.

Effective: 7 September 2026

Important health notice

Selfcare Scan is a cosmetic wellness and tracking tool for adults. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, prevent, or treat health conditions.

Information we process

  • Account data: email address, authentication identifier, and account timestamps. Supabase handles authentication credentials; Selfcare Scan does not store your plain-text password.
  • Skin profile data: skin type, concerns, allergy information, pregnancy or planning status, and onboarding status.
  • Scan data: capture time and source, cosmetic measurements for redness, blemishes, uneven tone, texture, and shine, analysis confidence, and notes.
  • Images: the face photo submitted for analysis and one small thumbnail of the latest scan.
  • Coach data: messages, recent conversation context, skin profile details, and current skin signals needed to answer.
  • Subscription data: app user identifier, entitlement status, and purchase history processed by RevenueCat and the applicable app store. We do not receive full payment-card details.
  • Technical data: service providers may process IP address, device, request, security, and diagnostic information needed to operate and protect their services.

Photos and AI processing

A full-size scan photo is transmitted through an authenticated Supabase Edge Function to Google Gemini to generate cosmetic measurements. The app deletes its temporary full-size capture after processing and does not save full-size photos in scan history.

For the latest report preview, the app creates a small JPEG thumbnail up to 160 pixels wide. It is stored in private Supabase Storage and replaces the previous thumbnail. Historical scan rows contain measurements, not images.

Google may retain or otherwise process API inputs and outputs for security, abuse prevention, and other purposes described in the Gemini API terms applicable to the service plan and region. Do not submit a photo you do not have permission to use.

How we use information

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Produce cosmetic skin reports and track changes over time.
  • Personalize routines, product guidance, and AI coach responses.
  • Sync your profile and measurements across signed-in devices.
  • Provide, verify, and restore subscription access.
  • Prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and protect the service.

Service providers

We disclose only the information needed to providers that operate the service on our behalf:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, private storage, and Edge Functions.
  • Google Gemini for scan analysis and AI coach responses.
  • RevenueCat for subscription entitlement and purchase-history processing.
  • Google Play or the Apple App Store for purchase and subscription billing.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

Storage and retention

Skin profile data and measurement history are kept in Supabase while your account remains active or until you reset that data. A local device cache may be used for continuity and migration. The latest thumbnail remains until it is replaced, you reset skin data, or the associated data is deleted.

Coach conversation history is not stored in the Selfcare Scan database in the current version. Recent messages are held while the coach screen is open and sent as context with a new question. Providers may retain operational records according to their terms and legal obligations.

Delete skin data and keep your account

In Selfcare Scan, open Account, select Reset skin data, and confirm. This deletes your skin profile, scan measurements, latest thumbnail, and related device cache while keeping your sign-in account.

Deletion starts immediately when you confirm. Selfcare Scan does not retain a copy of the deleted skin data. Your subscription and purchase records are not affected.

Delete your account and associated data

Sign in to Selfcare Scan, open Account, select Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm. This permanently deletes your sign-in account, skin profile, scan measurements, latest thumbnail, and related device cache. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot use the app, email 03ppcv@gmail.com from the email address used for the account. Never send your password. Identity verification may be required. Email requests are processed within 30 days.

Selfcare Scan does not retain a copy of deleted account or skin data. Google Play, the Apple App Store, RevenueCat, and payment providers may retain transaction records when required for billing, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription.

Security, transfers, and your rights

We use authenticated access, encrypted network connections, private storage, and per-account database rules. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Providers may process data in countries other than your own, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

Your privacy rights vary by location and may include access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection. You may also complain to the privacy regulator that applies where you live.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or providers change. The effective date will be revised when material updates are published. For privacy questions, email 03ppcv@gmail.com.
